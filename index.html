Fun Facts About Red Pandas

What Do Red Pandas Eat?

Bamboo

Red pandas primarily eat bamboo, which makes up most of their diet. They can consume up to 20,000 leaves a day.

Fruits

In addition to bamboo, red pandas enjoy various fruits, including apples, berries, and bananas.

Insects

Red pandas also eat insects such as termites, ants, and grubs.

Small Animals

Occasionally, red pandas may hunt small animals, including birds and rodents.

  • They are nocturnal animals.
  • Red pandas can climb trees with ease.
  • They have a special wrist bone that acts like a thumb.
